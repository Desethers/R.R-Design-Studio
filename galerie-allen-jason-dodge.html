Jason Dodge

3rd September, 2026 — 26th September, 2026

Press release

This exhibition contains several works that are not visible simultaneously. Each dedicated to someone living, or not.

One work, a grouping of flashing lights, is a constant presence throughout the duration of the exhibition, while other works are installed for a few days, and then exchanged until the entirety of the exhibition is replaced after September 26th.

This is the second exhibition by Jason Dodge in the gallery.
